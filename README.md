# WeaverLib 0.2.25

An initial rune and incantation mod for Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.256, and Java 21. By Ex.

## Build and install

Run `gradlew.bat build` on Windows or `./gradlew build` on Linux/macOS with a Java 21 JDK. Install the JAR from `build/libs` on the server and each client alongside [Thaumaturge 1.1.0 for NeoForge 1.21.1](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/thaumaturge/files/9452543), Curios, and Lithostitched. Gradle downloads the pinned development dependencies. For development launches, use `gradlew.bat runClient` or `gradlew.bat runServer` with Java 21. `build` also runs the standalone angel progression and payment checks (`angelRulesTest`).
## Angel form — first playable form

Becoming an angel requires **25 completed gameplay advancements**. This is an eligibility requirement; reaching it does not transform or awaken a player automatically. Operators can use `/weaverangel grant <player>` once eligible while the acquisition ritual is being designed. Completed advancements with a display count, including modded advancements; recipes and internal advancements without a display do not. Existing Sol owners retain their angel access and reserve. A newly awakened angel starts with a full reserve. The old Sun Stone item ID is retained for existing worlds.

Progression is completed advancements plus Prime blessings. Existing angels become **Archangels at 75** and **Seraphim at 150**, with four and six wings respectively. Their capacities are 300 and 525 Grace, and sunlight regeneration is 2.8 and 4.2 Grace per second (base Angel: 150 and 1.4). Rank promotions are permanent; increasing capacity does not refill the reserve. `/weaverangel status` shows your advancement, blessing, progression and rank totals.

Operators designate founding angels using `/weaverangel prime <player>`. This issues a token bound to that player; it does not bypass the 25-advancement entry requirement. Right-click another living angel with the token to grant **one progression point per Minecraft day**. Blessings are progression, not actual Minecraft advancement grants. The recipient’s new total cannot exceed the Prime’s current total, even within the same rank. Rejected blessings use no daily charge. The daily limit belongs to the Prime, so replacement or duplicate tokens do not bypass it. Progression, ownership and daily usage survive death and reconnecting. The day boundary uses the Overworld’s calendar, shared across dimensions.

Press **V** to open the invocation wheel. Click **Sanctus** (Divinity) three times to transform. The wings grow out from glowing points on the back over about 1.8 seconds: the inner wing extends first, then the wrists unfold and the outer feathers emerge. Orange embers and white-gold sparks follow their growth. Additional wing pairs emerge in a short stagger. This animation shares its server start time with other players and is dimmer outside sunlight. Repeat the sequence to revert. At Archangel rank, **Pyra → Pyra → Pyra** invokes the Inferno aspect. Its articulated wings have a substantial, layered deep crimson and burnt red-orange feather base with bright golden veins on every feather. There are no solid golden feathers: all vanes retain the muted crimson palette, while a narrow luminous shaft and curved gold branches trace each feather's barbs. The veins use the same growing, bending surface as the vanes in both camera views. The crimson feather pigment remains muted by 30%, with reduced edge glow, while the fire keeps its bright material. The solid vanes and fire share one feather layout. Hot flame meshes radiate from distributed points on both faces of the real vanes, rise through world-up buoyancy, inherit part of the wing motion, and respond to air drag, gentle wind, cooling and coherent turbulent eddies. They stay upright while hovering and bend behind the player during fast movement; rotating or diving does not rotate gravity with the body. Flame roots stay attached between simulation ticks, while the older gas drifts freely. Solid block collision shapes deflect the flow along walls and ceilings. Individual plumes have distinct animated patterns and fade over a short lifetime. The effect uses continuous crossed mesh ribbons and no native particles. A faint attached heat wash preserves the feather detail. Motion trails from primary-feather tips are now a lighter secondary effect that appears during movement. Feathers and flames share the growing, flapping, diving, wrapping and striking skeleton in both first and third person, with a little extra flame length during a strike. The surface aura is sparse and faint, with six additional animated wisps distributed from the player surface to one block away. All flame geometry and materials animate, including slash trails, the charging orb and the released beam. Inferno transformation, impacts, aerial attacks and dash effects are rendered as meshes and never spawn native particles. The same three-rune sequence returns you to human form; Sanctus three times switches directly back to Divinity. Entering Pyra costs 20 Grace; entering Divinity costs 5 (including the existing sunlight waiver). Switching preserves flight mode and movement, ends any ongoing charge/dash, and restarts wing growth. Pyra shares the flight controls but has its own expensive combat profile, described below. It does not grant fire immunity. **Nemesis** (Retribution) combinations remain unwritten. The six Divine/Pyra spells are listed below. Escape or V cancels an unfinished sequence. This is the only angel-specific keybind.

Feather bodies now write scene depth in a dedicated pass, so entities, water and clouds behind the wings are correctly hidden in first and third person, including Fabulous transparency composition. Soft feather fringes, light effects and fire use separate translucent passes, preserving the glow and the gaps between feathers. An optional EGL/OpenGL regression check is available at `tools/check_wing_depth.py`; it uses Minecraft's actual color and Fabulous shaders and is independent of the standard Java rule suite.

Divinity transformation costs 5 Grace when the combined energy supply can cover it. Sunlight waives this fee below 5 Grace to let an exhausted angel recover. Zero-energy transformation works under direct sunlight and immediately starts recovering energy. An attempt outside sunlight with insufficient combined energy produces a subdued flash and leaves you human. Human form never recharges. Angel form regenerates 1.4 Grace/second under direct daylight, up to 150. Grounded Divinity consumes 0.25 Grace/second. Total flight upkeep is 1.5 Grace/second in slow flight or 4.5 Grace/second in fast flight. Exhausting both the personal reserve and usable carried storage ends the transformation. Death, logout, dimension changes, and game-mode changes also restore human form and remove angelic flight privileges.

Wings use articulated shoulders, elbows and wrists with delayed feather motion, an extended power stroke and folded recovery. Creative-flight hovering flaps 44% slower than the initial cadence (a 30% reduction followed by another 20%); ascending smoothly speeds it up and descending slows it down (up to 40% either way). Wing roots sit close to the back. Blocking routes the inner wings behind and outside the arms and sleeves before curling the outer feathers forward into an overlapping X across the chest; ground attacks wind up and sweep outside the body. During a melee strike, the wing twists so its upper leading edge makes contact first and its feathers trail behind, then untwists during recovery. Fast-flight spin effects use the same diving body frame as the player, with layered gold and orange flame ribbons spiraling along the head-to-feet axis.

In angel form, double-tap jump for slow creative-style flight. Tap the normal sprint key while airborne to switch between slow and fast flight. The body and wings ease between upright and flying poses over about 0.4 seconds in either direction. Fast flight steers like continuously boosted Elytra flight and ends on landing or entering water. Descending into a dive smoothly folds the wings back along the body; they spread again as you level out or climb.

All mouse abilities require an empty main hand. **Divinity controls:** while grounded, left-click performs a randomized wing sweep with up to 4 blocks of reach for 2 Grace, and holding right-click crosses the wings as a directional shield. Blocking starts after five ticks, costs one Grace per prevented damage point, respects shield-bypassing attacks and piercing arrows, and axes disable it for five seconds. During slow flight, left-click launches a light feather for 3 Grace. These have bright white-gold vanes, a white shaft, a camera-facing glint and an extended fading trail for visibility from any viewing angle. Feather impacts show a small fire burst without a golden shockwave ring. Hold right-click to repel every nearby entity type within 4.5 blocks, including projectiles and minecarts, for one Grace per entity per second. During fast flight, **hold left-click** to channel a spinning charge with collision damage. The wings wrap around the player while charging. Activation costs **10 Grace**, followed by **10 Grace per second** in addition to normal fast-flight upkeep. Release left-click to stop. The channel also ends on insufficient energy, landing, entering water, switching flight mode, losing an empty main hand, or opening a screen. Carried Ember fuel can cover its energy costs. Each target can be hit once per uninterrupted charge. Hold right-click for a luminous mantle that reduces damage by 75% and spends one Grace per prevented damage point. An unaffordable action spends nothing and has no effect.

In **Pyra's grounded combat**, an empty-main-hand wing strike costs **15 Grace**, deals **15 damage**, and ignites struck enemies for five seconds. The striking flames stretch slightly and leave an animated fire slash in the air for about a second. There is **no grounded wing shield** in this aspect. While Pyra form is active, mobs within three blocks catch fire; players are excluded from this passive ignition.

**Hold right-click** with an empty main hand while not flying to cradle a growing fireball. Charging costs **20 Grace initially, then 50 Grace per second**, alongside normal upkeep; carried Ember stores can pay the shortfall. **Release right-click to fire** a beam with a flowing, golden-hot core and a turbulent red-orange flamethrower envelope. There is **no charge or power cap**. Damage grows linearly with invested energy; radius and reach grow with its square root, and the orb continues growing with its cube root. For reference: 20 Grace produces 24 damage, a 0.2-block radius and 12-block reach; 100 Grace produces 64 damage, a 1.4-block radius and 36-block reach, and charging past that keeps increasing all three. The release is a single damaging blast, with its animated fire lingering briefly afterward. The beam can hit multiple enemies along its width, stops at solid terrain or unloaded chunks, respects allies/PvP rules, and uses fire-tagged damage so normal fire immunity and resistance apply. It does not destroy or ignite blocks. A short recovery follows release. Opening a screen, changing held items, transforming, entering flight, losing the input lease, dying, or losing the form cancels the charge without firing; energy already invested is spent. An unaffordable charge tick adds no power. Exhausting energy still ends the transformation as usual.

**Pyra creative/hovering flight** uses normal Minecraft creative-flight speed (0.05 ability speed, compared with Divinity's slower 0.03). Switching directly between aspects updates this speed immediately. In this mode, left-click swings a random wing, which temporarily extends by about **50%**, and launches a broad crescent of animated fire after the strike's windup. This replaces Pyra's feather projectile. The arc costs **20 Grace**, deals **24 damage**, ignites targets for seven seconds, can pass through several enemies once each, and stops at solid terrain or unloaded chunks. The physical wing, its golden veins, attached flames and slash trail share the extended skeleton, including in first person.

Hold right-click during Pyra slow flight to form animated fireballs on the tips of **every wing**. Elbows move outside the arms, then wrists and tips smoothly gather in front over **18 ticks**; the tip orbs merge into one large fireball. Release to launch with an explosion sound. A quick tap still completes the gathering animation before launch, without spending further charge energy after release. It costs **30 Grace initially plus 60 Grace per second held**. Invested Grace increases damage linearly, blast radius with the square root and orb size with the cube root, without a charge cap. At 30 Grace it deals up to **32 damage** in a **2.5-block radius**; at 130 Grace, up to **80 damage** in a **6-block radius**. Damage falls off toward the edge and respects terrain exposure, allies, PvP, fire resistance and immunity. The travelling orb sweeps for entities and blocks; the impact produces animated volumetric fire, damage and knockback, and **never damages or ignites blocks**. Switching between grounded/slow/fast modes, changing items, opening a screen or losing the form cancels charging without firing.

**Pyra fast flight:** the held dash costs **30 Grace to start plus 15 per second**, plus **10 Grace per successfully damaged enemy**. Each enemy can be hit once per uninterrupted dash for **30 damage**, followed by six seconds of burning. Immune/canceled hits cost nothing; if the hit fee cannot be paid, the dash passes without damaging or knocking back that enemy. A block collision ends the dash and costs another **40 Grace** for a terrain-damaging explosion (radius 3, normal block resistance/drops and NeoForge explosion hooks). Collision damage is canceled only when its protection can be paid: **2 Grace per damage point**. The short impact protection window covers the collision that immediately follows a detected wall impact. Unaffordable protection does not grant immunity. Unlike the charged fireball, this dash explosion **can break blocks**. The fast-flight mantle still reduces damage by 75%, but costs **3 Grace per prevented damage point** in Pyra.

Pyra upkeep, including its passive ignition aura, is **2 Grace/second grounded**, **8/second in slow flight**, or **14/second in fast flight**. Attacks and charging costs are additional. Capacity, sunlight regeneration and carried Ember conversion rates remain rank-based as before. All Pyra fire effects use animated mesh materials; no native fire or explosion particles are spawned by these attacks. Install matching 0.2.25 versions on server and clients because the effect and charge payloads changed.

### Divine/Pyra wheel spells

D is **Sanctus / Divinity** and P is **Pyra / Inferno**. Order matters. **DDD** and **PPP** retain their transformation/toggle behavior; these six spells complete all eight D/P combinations. Every mixed spell contains Pyra and therefore requires **Archangel or Seraphim** under the existing rune unlock rules. Take either angel form before invoking a mixed spell. Spells work with held items; mouse wing attacks still require an empty main hand. Before the third wheel click, the hovered rune previews the spell name and cost.

| Combination | Spell | Grace | Effect |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| DDP | Healing Flames | 15 | A moving 3-block aura heals the caster and all nearby living entities for **1 heart each second for 4 seconds**. Recasting refreshes the aura rather than stacking it. |
| DPP | Smite | 25 | Aim at a creature or solid terrain within **32 blocks**. A narrow beam of burning light descends onto that point, dealing **10 × angel rank** damage (10/20/30 for Angel/Archangel/Seraphim) and igniting enemies. The existing Pyra gate makes 20 the lowest accessible damage. Indoor ceilings limit the visible column. |
| DPD | Repel the Wicked | 20 | A spherical shockwave expands to **6 blocks over 12 ticks**, pushing all nearby entity types, including creatures, other players, projectiles, dropped items and vehicles. Each entity is pushed once. Incoming projectiles are sent outward at least 2.2 blocks/tick. |
| PDD | Cleansing Flames | 15 | Within **3 blocks**, removes harmful status effects from living entities, including the caster. Every affected player also loses up to **3 temporary Warp and 1 normal Warp**. Neither can go below zero; permanent Warp is untouched. |
| PPD | Circle of Flame | 35 | A stationary burning disk expands to **6 blocks over 10 ticks** and lasts **20 ticks total**. It pushes entities outward and deals **8 fire damage each server tick** to enemies remaining inside. The caster is excluded. A dedicated damage type bypasses the usual hit cooldown while preserving armor, fire immunity/resistance and damage events. |
| PDP | Divine Forge | 8/input item | Instantly applies the held stack's **normal smelting recipe**, including modded recipes and their output components/remainders. Smelts as many input items as the available Grace plus carried Ember can afford. Unprocessed items stay in hand; outputs enter inventory or drop safely if full. Empty or unsmeltable items cost nothing. |

Healing includes nearby creatures even when hostile. Offensive spells respect existing ally/PvP checks; repulsion affects all entity types. Smite and Circle of Flame never destroy or ignite blocks. The new effects layer animated gold light with the existing mesh flame shader, without native particles. Insufficient combined energy prevents an action; invalid Smite targets and forge inputs are not charged. Ongoing auras/waves end when the caster dies, reverts to human, leaves the dimension, logs out or loses the form. Install matching **0.2.25** server/client versions for the new effect channel. The remaining **19** three-rune combinations contain Retribution and remain unwritten.

Operators can directly select `/weaverangel stage angel`, `/weaverangel stage archangel`, or `/weaverangel stage seraphim`; append a player name to target someone else. These testing overrides bypass the entry requirement and persist across reconnects. Use `/weaverangel stage auto [player]` to restore natural progression; a previously human tester below the entry requirement returns to human. Changing an existing angel’s stage keeps and clamps their Grace rather than refilling it. A human entering a testing stage starts full. `/weaverangel status` identifies an active override.

Operators can use `/weaverangel refill`, `/weaverangel energy <amount>`, and `/weaverangel remove` for testing. Angelic energy is stored separately from Thaumaturge Vis and Goety Soul Energy. The previous Sol-specific Warp purification and casting penalties have been retired. Embers Re-Ignited is supported as an optional external fuel source.

With **Embers Re-Ignited** installed, batteries and storage devices in the player inventory (including hotbar, armor and offhand) cover missing angel energy. Personal Grace pays first, then supported carried Ember stores in slot order. This applies to transformation, upkeep, wing attacks, feathers, fire arcs, charged beams/fireballs, repulsion, dash impacts and damage absorbed by defenses. Default exchange: **100 Ember = 1 Grace**; change `emberPerGrace` in `weaverlib-angels-server.toml` to balance it. Payment can span multiple items, is all-or-nothing, and does not recharge the personal reserve. At zero Grace, usable carried energy can sustain angel form. Without enough combined energy the action fails; failed upkeep reverts the player to human. Embers is optional.

## Rune guide

The canvas key requires the same Warp unlock as rune casting. Operators can use `/weaverrunes unlockall` for themselves or `/weaverrunes unlockall <player>` for one online player. The grant is saved on that server and reveals every rune in the guide. Canvas access still follows the Warp unlock.

Draw a rune freehand and finish it with **Tab** or release the canvas key. A recognized rune is saved in your guide for this Minecraft profile on this installation. Open the guide with **G** and scroll through learned runes.

Right-click the canvas to center a copy of the selected rune at the pointer. Drag away from the click to enlarge it and release to place it. Use the middle mouse button if right-click is already used by the canvas. Placed runes use the same spell rules as drawings. **Ctrl+Z** removes a placed rune in one step.

## Casting

| Incantation | Effect on the caster | Cost |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| `incant lux` | 60 seconds of night vision | 5 Vis |
| `incant sana` | Heals 2 hearts | 8 Vis |

Type incantations in regular chat with no slash. If the first word is `incant` (case-insensitive), only players within 10 blocks in the same dimension receive the message, even if the spell fails. Casting is available to living, non-spectator players who have earned the Warp unlock. A locked player who types a valid incantation gets a private chat message, and the attempted incantation is suppressed.

Hold **K** to open the parchment canvas. Press **G** to show or hide the rune guide, then scroll to browse. The numbers mark the start of each stroke and the arrows show its direction. The right page shows the latest rune match, and a quiet page-turn sound plays when you press Tab to finish a rune, toggle the guide, or scroll to another guide.

- **Lux** uses a V drawn from upper left, down, then up to the right. Draw the crossbar left to right.
- **Sana** starts with a rising, kinked stem that turns right at the top. From the middle of the stem, draw a short branch toward the lower right. Finish with a detached clockwise ring, starting at the top.

Drag with the left mouse button, or the right mouse button if you bind the canvas to left click. Release the canvas key to cast. Your drawing and its rune settings will be there when you reopen the canvas. Use **Ctrl+Z** to undo one stroke, or **Esc** to clear the drawing and close without casting. The drawing is kept for the current game session. Ordered Lux and Sana matches have full potency. Recognizable Lux and Sana shapes drawn out of order can cast at half potency for the same Vis cost.

The server recognizes the submitted ink and runs the same spell logic for both inputs. Vis comes from Thaumaturge's aura in the caster's current chunk. Insufficient Vis, unknown inputs, full-health healing and redundant night vision leave the aura unspent. There is a shared one-second interval between attempts, and native spells also use Thaumaturge’s computed cooldown. Lux and Sana each accept up to three optional modifiers.

## Temporary Warp and casting costs

A drawn or spoken spell generates **1 Temporary Warp** once its initial Vis payment succeeds. Drawn and spoken spells always cost **3× normal Vis**, regardless of how much Temporary Warp you have. When a WeaverLib Warp gain would take you above **100 Temporary Warp**, you gain **1 Normal Warp** and Temporary Warp resets to **1**, discarding any overflow.

Leap surcharges and Eternal upkeep for drawn and spoken spells also cost **3× Vis**. Automatic repeats generate no additional Warp. Inscribe generates **20 Temporary Warp** after successfully binding an item and costs **150 Vis**. Item activation uses its own battery pricing, and Erase generates no Warp.

## Unlocking rune and word casting

Reach **50 combined Normal and Permanent Warp** or become a **Goety lich** to unlock both casting methods. Existing liches qualify when they next join or tick on the server. An explicit operator lock takes priority. Temporary Warp and bonuses from equipment do not count. The unlock is saved to the player and survives death, reconnecting, and purification. On a natural unlock, a two-part message appears above the hotbar. A locked rune attempt reports your current progress without spending Vis.

The server config entry `requiredWarp` in `weaverlib-server.toml` controls the threshold. Set it to `0` to disable the Warp requirement. Changing the threshold affects players who have yet to earn the unlock.

Operators can type `/weaverwarp unlock` or `/weaverwarp lock` for themselves. Add an online player name to target someone else. Both arguments must be typed manually. A manual lock takes precedence over the threshold and lasts until an operator unlocks that player.

## Lux and Sana rune slots

Draw the main effect near the center. Press **Tab** between complete runes to mark the end of a glyph. Draw smaller modifiers around the effect. They are read clockwise from the top, with nearer runes first at the same angle. Main effects can be traced on the canvas, and modifier guides appear on the right page.

| Slot | Default | Modifier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form | Affect the selected target | **Pulse** affects up to eight eligible targets within three blocks of the selected target, visible to the caster. |
| Target | Caster | **Gaze** selects the living entity you're looking at, up to eight blocks away. |
| Power | Base effect | **Surge** multiplies duration or healing by 1.5 and rounds its Vis multiplier up per recipient. |

**Null** skips one slot and uses its default. Trailing slots can be omitted. For example, draw Lux in the center, Null above it, and Gaze to its right to give another player night vision. For stronger self-healing, draw Sana with Null, Null, Surge in clockwise order.

To cast Pulse, draw Lux or Sana first and press **Tab**. Draw a small heartbeat line above it with a short lead-in, a sharp rise and dip, and a rise halfway back into a short tail. Pulse goes in the Form slot. Place Gaze and Surge clockwise after it. A rune placed just to either side of twelve o’clock still starts the clockwise order.

Lux affects players. Sana can heal living targets. Pulse charges per affected target plus 2 Vis. A malformed spell, missing target, or insufficient Vis leaves the aura unspent. Typed incantations use the effect's defaults.

## Thaumaturge focus magic

Native effects, deliveries, modifiers, and flows have Latin-inspired chat words and drawn runes. For example, `incant cura auge fulgor` casts Heal through Amplify and Beam. Old English spell words still work. See the [component and argument reference](docs/thaumaturge-components.md) for the full catalog, spell construction, settings, and aspect choices.

## Inscribed items

Hold a single item in your main hand. Draw **Inscribe** first, press **Tab**, then draw the spell normally. Put the spell's main rune near the center and its other runes clockwise around it. Inscribe's position is ignored when ordering the stored spell. Once you have drawn **Inscribe**, open the guide with **G** and scroll to it to review its three strokes. You can also say `incant insculpe cura auge fulgor` to bind Heal + Amplify + Beam. The earlier word `inscribe` still works.

Inscribe requires the Warp unlock and costs **150 Vis** from the local aura. It binds the spell without casting it. An item can hold one inscription. Its tooltip shows the stored incantation or rune names, the battery charge, and the casting multiplier.

A new battery starts empty and holds **200 Vis**. An item with a native Vis battery keeps its existing charge and capacity. Charge it with Thaumaturge's **Recharge Pedestal** or **Vis Amulet**. Right-click the pedestal to place the item on it. Inscribed items do not refill directly from ambient Vis while held.

Right-click with the item in your main hand to cast from its battery. Sneak-right-click to use the item normally. Using the item does not require the Warp unlock. Thaumaturge's normal spell research requirements still apply. A cast costs **1.5×** its normal Vis cost, or **1.2×** if a loaded recipe for that item requires a thaumium or void-metal ingredient. Thaumaturge stores whole Vis charges, so costs round up. Inscribed Eternal pulses and Leap surcharges also draw from that item's battery.

Vanilla map, written-book, and banner copying recipes reject inscribed items so charging one item cannot create multiple batteries.

With Curios installed, press **J** (Cast Inscribed Curio) to use the first inscribed item in Curios' active equipment order. It uses the same spell, cooldown, and battery cost as a held inscription. If the first item's battery cannot pay, the key does not move on to another item. Cosmetic and inactive slots are skipped.

To clear a stored spell, hold the item in your main hand and draw **Erase** by itself, or say `incant dele`. Erasing requires the Warp unlock and costs no Vis. It removes the battery added by Inscribe and discards any charge in it. An item's existing battery and charge are kept. The item can then be inscribed again.

## Optional Goety casting

With Goety 3.2.x installed on the client and server, hold a Goety wand or staff with an Osseous Focus equipped. Say `incant goety osseous` or draw the **Osseous** rune by itself. The rune traces a diagonal bone in one continuous stroke. Goety casts use their own focus, casting time, cooldown, summon rules and resource costs. Goety and Thaumaturge components cannot share an incantation, rune cast or inscription.

Goety's stored Soul Energy pays first. Each missing unit takes one percentage point of personal soul. For example, an eight-energy cast with three stored spends those three and leaves a full-soul player at **95%**. The cast is blocked if the total cost would leave less than **1%**. Spending personal soul generates Temporary Warp at an increasing rate as the reserve drops, rising by one Warp per point spent for each 20 percentage points missing. The existing condensation threshold applies.

Personal soul scales maximum health, attack damage, movement speed, attack speed and world color saturation. It recovers by **one percentage point every 30 seconds of active play**. Goety Soul Energy gains repair the personal reserve first and deposit the remainder in native storage. Recovery restores your health cap and leaves current health at its existing value.
